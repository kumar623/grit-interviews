GRIT Track Badge interview scheduling

Write-up for the working prototype at https://grit-web-production.up.railway.app (one-tap demo sign-in for Student, Interviewer and Interview team; password for every demo account is grit-demo-2026). Stack: Supabase (Postgres, Auth, row level security, Realtime) and Railway.

1. States of a student's interview journey

State is kept per student per track and is derived in one database function from skills, bookings, outcomes and flags, so the student screen, the team table and the booking rules can never disagree.

Eligible, not bookedAll skill levels for the badge cleared and no live booking. Also re-entered after a cancel, a retake, a first no-show, or when a cooldown ends.Student confirms a slot → Booked.

2. Edge cases handled, and assumptions

  • Two students, one slot, same instant. The database lets exactly one through (section 3). The other sees the slot-taken message and fresh times; nothing is half-booked.
  • Double click or two tabs. A second booking by the same student for the same track is rejected by a unique index, even if both requests arrive together.
  • Eligible for two tracks. One live booking per track, and two bookings for the same student can't share a start time.
  • Late changes. Students can reschedule or cancel until 12 h before; after that the buttons are disabled and the reason is shown.
  • Interviewer can't make it. A booked slot can't be silently removed from availability. The interviewer cancels with a reason; the student is told, loses no attempt, and can rebook.
  • Outcome never recorded. The slot shows "Needs outcome", the interviewer gets a banner, and the student appears under "Needs attention" for the team.
  • Wrong outcome recorded. Editable for 24 h (pass can become fail and the badge is withdrawn, or the reverse). Every change is in the audit log with the previous value.
  • Malpractice flagged later. Works from any state, including after a pass: badge and Miles are removed, upcoming interviews are cancelled and the slot is released.
  • Feedback versus private notes. Students see feedback only. Private notes sit in a schema the API does not expose.
  • Students who have not booked. Team view shows days since eligible, flags 7+ days, and logs reminders.
  • No capacity. Team view compares open slots in the next 7 days with students still to book. Students with no open times get a clear empty state.
  • Time zones. Stored in UTC, always shown in IST with the label.

Assumptions to confirm with the GRIT team (tracks, badges and skill levels follow grit.niatindia.com; the AI Product Mastery Novice skill list is the published example, the AI Systems Mastery list is a placeholder): 30-minute one-to-one interviews on Google Meet; students pick a time, not an interviewer, and any interviewer can assess any track; 14-day cooldown and 3 attempts per track per season; Miles per badge follow GRIT's own tables, and a nullified badge's Miles are removed; reminders go out by the existing GRIT notification channel (the prototype only logs them); appeals are handled by people, not the tool.

How two students are stopped from booking the same slot. The browser never writes to tables. Every write is a database function, and reads are limited by row level security. Booking is one transaction with three layers:

-- 1. Lock one open slot at the chosen time (interviewers are pooled); a locked row is skipped, not waited on
select id from slots where starts_at = $1 and status = 'open' limit 1 for update skip locked;   -- none found → SLOT_TAKEN
-- 2. Flip it only if it is still open
update slots set status = 'booked' where id = $slot and status = 'open';                        -- 0 rows → SLOT_TAKEN
insert into bookings (slot_id, student_id, track_id, attempt_no) values (...);
-- 3. Backstops that hold even if application code is wrong
create unique index one_live_booking_per_slot on bookings (slot_id) where status in ('booked','completed','no_show');
create unique index one_open_booking_per_student_track on bookings (student_id, track_id) where status = 'booked';

The loser's transaction rolls back completely and they see: "That slot was just booked by someone else. Here are the nearest open times." Open times also update live on every screen through Supabase Realtime, so most students never reach the race at all.

Tested against the live database, with real sign-ins: 20 simultaneous bookings on one slot gave 1 booked, 19 rejected, 1 row. Four different students booking the last free slot at one time gave 1 booked, 3 rejected. One student firing two bookings at once gave 1 booked, 1 rejected. The interview team can rerun the first test from the Race test tab.

4. The three messages that matter most to a student

You're ready for your Level Clearance Interview
You've cleared every skill level for the Novice Badge in AI Product Mastery. Book your interview to earn the badge and its Miles.  [Book interview]
Interview booked for Thu, 12 Nov, 4:00 PM IST
Your join link opens 10 minutes before. You can reschedule until Thu, 12 Nov, 4:00 AM.
You didn't clear the AI Product Mastery interview this time
Your skill levels stay cleared, and you can book again from 26 Nov. (followed by the interviewer's feedback)

Why these three: the first is the only prompt that starts the flow, the second removes every "when, where, can I change it" question, and the third is the moment a student is most likely to give up, so it leads with what they keep and when they can try again. Pass, retake, cancelled-by-interviewer, slot-taken and nullified messages are also in the prototype.
